Chapter 4: An Unexpected Meeting

In the square, the old woman found him again—surrounded by dogs and pigeons, a boy folded into himself. She sat beside him and listened. Not to fix. To witness.

She spoke of the butterfly effect, of small acts echoing outward: a glass of water, a hand to a stray, a seed in a pot, a smile that catches light in another life. He laughed for the first time in days. The city hadn’t changed, but his angle on it had.

He left with a new secret, twin to the bees’ warning: happiness grows where kindness is planted and protected.
