Chapter 6: Krawzer, the Realm of Dreams

Between waking and sleep he fell into another world—Krawzer—where magic replaced machine. Seized by villagers, tested by stones, he touched a crystal and golden light flooded the clearing. They called him a bearer of light, a healer.

The power surged beyond his control, mending fields and bodies while he slept. When he woke, Geo Lumis offered him belonging, and twins named Tio and Lia taught him to speak. He learned that light in this world balanced shadow; that choice mattered; that some places feared what he carried.

He stayed. For now, belonging was a kind of medicine too.
