Chapter 5: The Dystopia

Grounded by his grandparents, Gael turned his room into a sanctuary. Walls bloomed with forests and rivers drawn by hand; the floor filled with origami birds and small paper bees. He wrote a diary that doubled as a manifesto—plans for urban gardens, for shelters, for teaching others to care.

At night he logged into the game and played a High Priest of Light. He healed, shielded, resurrected. People gathered around that light. Their courage rose when he did. He began to suspect the note the universe was trying to play through him was the same on both sides of the screen.

In the real world, there is no respawn. All the more reason to carry the light.
